The following is the interview script. Note that we frame the interview neutrally, and not leading.

1. Could you walk me through your typical content creation process? What role do generative AI tools play in your workflow?

2. When you utilize these AI tools, what kinds of digital markers or traces do you notice being associated with your outputs?

3. What are your thoughts regarding the practices of applying AI labels or provenance tracking to AI-generated content?

4. From your perspective, what are these labels' benefits, especially regarding privacy and security?

5. From your perspective, what are these labels' risks, especially regarding privacy and security?

6. What are your thoughts on the types of information potentially embedded within these tracking mechanisms?

7. How do you perceive the automated systems used by platforms to detect, flag, or display AI-generated content? What outcomes do you anticipate once content is identified as AI-generated?

8. When preparing your content for final distribution or publication, what actions, if any, do you take regarding the labels or markers generated by AI tools?

9. Can you describe specific scenarios where you choose to keep these system-generated or system-added labels? What guides that choice?

10. Do you ever choose to proactively add your own labels, watermarks, or embedded indicators to your content? What leads you to do so?

11. For any modifications you perform, if applicable, could you describe the specific technical steps or tools involved in your workflow?

12. If any, how do different professional or social environments influence how you handle these labels?

13. If any, what do you expect to happen to the evaluation of your work as a result of these adjustments?
